Esteemed visitors of this repository, 

Greetings.

I sincerely hope this program finds you well.

It is my great pleasure to accept the invitation from an outstanding founding member to serve as a successor to this repository, which displays these organized documents related to the Computer Science major in the City University of Hong Kong system. 

We genuinely appreciate our classmates' and friends' selfless and generous help: there is no denying that we could not have completed this repository without them. Therefore, we sincerely seek the marvellous successors to maintain the project.

Moreover, I am deeply aware that it has been less than one academic year since I became the successor to this repository. Considering this, I am humbled with reverence and awe. If you find mistakes in this repository, please help us correct them. 

Furthermore, I would like to give you some special reminders: Using these materials for commercial purposes without permission, or directly plagiarizing them without any edits, are not phenomena we are willing to see. 

In conclusion, we truly appreciate your visit. What's more, we are also genuinely looking forward to the participation of new outstanding members.

Yours Faithfully,

Chongyu Chang
